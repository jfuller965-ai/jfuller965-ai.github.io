Privacy Policy — Ninebank Publisher

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Ninebank Publisher is a tool that helps a video creator send their own finished videos to their own TikTok account. Uploaded videos arrive in the creator's TikTok account as unpublished drafts, so the creator can review each one inside TikTok and choose whether and when to post it. This policy explains what information the tool handles and how it is used.

Information we handle

TikTok account information. When you connect your TikTok account, TikTok's Login Kit shares a small amount of basic profile information with the tool: your TikTok user ID, your display name, and your avatar image. We use this only to confirm which account is connected and to show you that confirmation in the tool.

Video files you choose to upload. When you select a video file, the tool sends that file — along with any title or description you provide — to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API, addressed to your own account. Video files are transmitted for upload and are not kept or stored by us after the upload completes.

We do not collect anything else. The tool does not track you, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and does not read your TikTok content, messages, followers, or activity.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the tool: to sign you in, to show you which of your accounts is connected, and to deliver the videos you select to your own TikTok account as drafts. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party, other than TikTok itself, which receives the account information and video files needed to complete the uploads you request.

Disconnecting your account

You can disconnect Ninebank Publisher from your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Manage apps / authorized apps). Once disconnected, the tool can no longer access your account or upload on your behalf.

Data security and retention

Account information is retained only while your account is connected, so the tool can function. If you disconnect the app or contact us to ask for deletion, we will delete any account information associated with you.

Children

Ninebank Publisher is intended for creators who hold a TikTok account and are old enough to use TikTok under TikTok's own terms. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
